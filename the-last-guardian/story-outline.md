# THE LAST GUARDIAN

## Project Overview
An original African superhero action film.

## Main Character
Name: Kairo
Age: 29
Appearance: Dark-skinned, athletic African man.
Costume: Futuristic black armour with bronze African patterns.
Power: Electric-blue energy and protective shields.

## Story
A futuristic African city comes under attack by a mysterious shadow creature.

Kairo arrives to defend the city.

He declares: "You chose the wrong city."

He fights the creature using his blue energy shield.

But just when he thinks he has won, a much larger shadow appears.

A mysterious voice warns:
"You have no idea what is coming."

## Film Style
Realistic 3D animation.

## Language
English.

## Target Duration
30 seconds.
