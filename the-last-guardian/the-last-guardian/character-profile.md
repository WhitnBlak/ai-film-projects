# KAIRO – CHARACTER PROFILE

## Basic Information
Name: Kairo
Age: 29
Role: African superhero and protector.

## Physical Appearance
- Dark-skinned African man.
- Athletic, muscular build.
- Short, neat black hair.
- Strong facial features.
- Realistic human proportions.

## Costume
- Futuristic matte-black body armour.
- Elegant bronze African geometric patterns.
- Glowing electric-blue symbol on his chest.
- Blue energy surrounding his hands.

## Superpowers
- Generates electric-blue energy.
- Creates protective energy shields.
- Exceptional strength and combat ability.

## Personality
- Calm and courageous.
- Intelligent and strategic.
- Protective of innocent people.
- Never abandons his city.

## Visual Style
Realistic 3D animation.
Cinematic Hollywood-style lighting.
Original African superhero design.
